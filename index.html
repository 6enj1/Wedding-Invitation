The Beginning

Our story began in 2013, when a mutual friend introduced us. What seemed like a simple conversation was already part of something greater, as God was quietly writing our story long before we could understand it.

A Growing Friendship

Over time, what felt ordinary became something deeper. We were always there for each other, through both the small and meaningful moments. Little by little, a strong bond formed, growing stronger with each passing year and bringing us closer in a way that felt natural and undeniable.

The First Time We Met

In 2015, in Johannesburg, we saw each other for the first time. It was a simple moment, yet it carried something special. Deep down, there was a quiet certainty, as if our hearts already knew this moment would lead to something far greater.

A Love Written by God

With patience and perfect timing, God nurtured within us a love that is sincere, steady, and true. Through the years, he shaped us and gently drew us closer together.

In time, everything became clear. There was no one better for us, no one else we could imagine building our lives with. Today, our bond has grown so strong that we cannot imagine our lives without each other.

It was all part of God's plan, bringing our lives together to build a love meant to last a lifetime.

A Place That Holds Meaning

It is only natural that we have chosen Johannesburg to begin this lifelong journey together, a place that holds the memory of the first time we saw each other, a moment simple in appearance, yet deeply meaningful to us.

It would be a true joy for us to share this special day with you.
