The Beginning — 2013

Our story began in 2013, when a mutual friend introduced us… though even then, God was already at work, writing something greater than we could see.

The First Meeting — 2015

What started as a simple conversation grew, over time, into a sincere and lasting friendship. In 2015, in Johannesburg, we saw each other for the first time, unaware that this moment would mark the beginning of something far more meaningful.

A Love Written by God
